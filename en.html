Petit Carnet Privacy Policy

Version dated 28 October 2026

Your records stay on your iPhone. You choose whether to enable iCloud, share a record or save a copy to a file.

Who is responsible?

Lionel Pereira is responsible for Petit Carnet. For questions about your data or your child’s data, contact yoonnel@mailo.com.

Information in your record

Petit Carnet helps you track the growth and vaccinations of a child you are responsible for or authorised to support.

The record contains their first name, date of birth, sex and, if entered, expected due date and birth weight. Measurements include their date, weight, height, head circumference, source and your notes.

Vaccination tracking saves the checkboxes you select for each child. It does not record injection dates, batch numbers or professionals. This information may constitute health data.

On your iPhone

Profiles, measurements and vaccination checkboxes are saved on your iPhone and remain available offline. Your preferences are also kept on this device.

You do not need a Petit Carnet account or password. The app uses technical identifiers to distinguish records and their data.

With iCloud

If you enable iCloud, profiles, measurements and vaccination checkboxes are sent to Apple’s iCloud service linked to your Apple Account. You can access them on devices using that account.

Sync works while the app is open and online. Without a connection, changes stay on your iPhone until they can be sent.

You can pause sync in Settings → Data and iCloud. This does not delete data already stored on the iPhone or in iCloud.

Sync also sends deletions. It does not provide a history for restoring earlier versions of a record.

With relatives

Sharing only covers the record you choose: its profile, measurements, notes and vaccination checkboxes.

Each invited person uses their own Apple Account. The owner manages participants and permissions from the record menu → Share record. Apple handles invitations and information needed for sharing.

When you revoke access, new changes are blocked as soon as the app detects the revocation. This does not remotely erase copies already on the recipient’s device, exported files or screenshots.

A copy in a file

In Settings → Data and iCloud, Save a copy of the record collects a child’s profile, measurements and vaccination checkboxes in one file. Sharing permissions are not included.

You choose the location in Files, such as On My iPhone to keep it outside iCloud. This file is not encrypted and does not update automatically. Keep it somewhere private.

Restore a copy adds a separate record after confirmation. Existing records are not replaced. The restored record syncs with your iCloud if this feature is enabled.

Reading with the camera

With your permission, the camera can read the printed name on a vaccine box or label. Images are processed on your device, with no photo saved and nothing sent to an external service.

Scanned text is used temporarily to find a name in the app’s selection. It is not added to the record or kept in logs. Scanning does not tick any boxes: you confirm the doses received yourself.

App lock

You can enable app lock in Settings. It uses Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Petit Carnet receives the verification result, never your biometric data.

Services used

Petit Carnet has no advertising or dedicated audience analytics tool. Apple provides iCloud for sync and sharing under its own terms and privacy policy.

Links to public sources and documents open their publishers’ websites. Those sites apply their own privacy policies.

The contact button prepares an email in your mail app. You decide whether to send it. The app version is added to the draft, without record data.

Retention and your choices

Data is kept on your iPhone and, if you enable sync, in iCloud. Copies saved in Files remain in the chosen location until deleted.

Depending on your permissions, you can view and edit information, delete measurements, save a copy and revoke access you granted. Deleting data in the app does not erase previously exported files.

For requests about your or your child’s data, contact Lionel Pereira, responsible for Petit Carnet, at yoonnel@mailo.com. Messages are kept as long as needed to handle your request and follow-up.

Subject to applicable conditions, you can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, or object to certain processing. Verification of your identity or authority over the child’s data may be required. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).
